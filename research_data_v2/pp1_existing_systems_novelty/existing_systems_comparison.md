## Interpretation for the PP1 slide

These systems already support important parts of the user journey: discovery, reviews, budget information, personalized suggestions, inquiries and, in some cases, booking. PlanoVision should therefore claim **a transparent research integration of explicit event constraints, bounded multi-vendor combination search, recorded ranking factors and uncertainty-aware explanations**, not invention of vendor recommendation or wedding planning.

The comparison is limited to reviewed public material. It is neither a feature-complete audit of these services nor a head-to-head experiment. A rigorous commercial baseline would require controlled access, comparable vendor data and the same event inputs.

## Source handling

All four rows rely on official provider pages. Some pages are promotional and can change; the access date and exact URLs above make the claims inspectable. Provider statements about availability mean information visible on their platform, not an independent guarantee of live calendars.

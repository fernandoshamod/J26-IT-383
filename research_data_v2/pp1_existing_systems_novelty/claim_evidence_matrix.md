Read alongside [commercial comparison](./existing_systems_comparison.md), [peer-reviewed literature](./academic_literature_review.md), and [source audit](./research_gap_and_actual_contribution.md). “Novelty” here is an inspectable domain integration and proposed evaluation question; it is **not** a claim of a new Random Forest, DFS or weighted-score algorithm.

| Commercial vendor directories with search, reviews and personalization | Public sources do not describe an auditable end-to-end combination algorithm or uncertainty record. Their internal systems remain unknown. | Expose each filter, combination, rank factor and pending check for an event request. | [Candidate selection](../../backend/engine/candidate_selection.py), [bounded search](../../backend/engine/bounded_package_generator.py), [explanation](../../backend/engine/package_explanations.py). | Same events and data in a representative directory or transparent reproduction; record coverage, cost, time, usefulness. | Workflow implemented; no controlled commercial comparison. | Commercial feature absence cannot be asserted. |

## Presentation-safe novelty claim

“PlanoVision implements an **auditable event-vendor package recommendation prototype**: it filters recorded synthetic service options by the event, searches bounded distinct-vendor combinations, ranks examined packages by declared cost, review-rule and category criteria, and exposes explanations, no-match causes and unknown verification needs. A separate synthetic conflict demo illustrates evidence-based pair checks. The scientific question is whether this integrated, uncertainty-aware workflow improves decision quality and transparency over explicit baselines; that comparative validation is future work.”

Avoid saying “first,” “more accurate than commercial platforms,” “predicts reliable vendors,” “fully compatible vendors,” or “optimal package.”

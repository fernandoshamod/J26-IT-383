## Synthesis

The papers establish that multi-criterion selection, multiple suppliers, package recommendation, explanation and constrained scheduling are established research areas. PlanoVision’s defensible **research contribution** is a domain-specific, auditable combination of these ideas under explicit evidence limits. It cannot claim that bounded DFS, Random Forest, weighted ranking or explanations are individually new algorithms.

The strongest proposed evaluation compares: (1) a reproducible cost-only or single-vendor baseline, (2) constrained package generation with and without risk/category factors, (3) bounded search against exhaustive search on tractable subsets, and (4) explanation faithfulness and user comprehension. No such new head-to-head results were generated for this study.

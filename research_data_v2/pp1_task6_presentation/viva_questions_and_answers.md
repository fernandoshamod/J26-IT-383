# PP1 viva: grounded technical questions and short answers

1. **What is the component scope?** Vendor option selection, dynamic package assembly, risk/compatibility signals, bounded ranking, explanations and simulated feasibility. Venue Suitability/Digital Twin is external. [Implementation](./implementation_summary.md).
2. **Are packages loaded from a saved catalog?** The dynamic path reads current service-option records and assembles combinations at request time. Historical research pages do not supply them. [Dynamic service](../../backend/engine/dynamic_package_service.py).
3. **Does every repeat request yield different vendors?** No. It gets a new USER ID, while deterministic selection can choose the same options for identical inputs. [Task 5 repeated request](../pp1_task5_demo_readiness/demo_readiness_report.md).
4. **What makes a candidate eligible?** Requested service, supported event type, synthetic location/capacity and other recorded constraints. [Candidate selector](../../backend/engine/candidate_selection.py).
5. **How do you avoid combinatorial explosion?** At most 12 candidates/service and 5,000 search nodes by default; truncation is disclosed. [Historical configuration](../../research_evaluation/evaluation_summary.json).
6. **Is the best package globally optimal?** No. It is highest ranked in the examined bounded pool. [Generator](../../backend/engine/bounded_package_generator.py).
7. **Can a vendor appear twice in one package?** The generator excludes repeated vendor IDs for complete required-service combinations. [Generator](../../backend/engine/bounded_package_generator.py).
8. **What are ranking weights?** Production cost 0.4, review-rule risk favorability 0.4, category compatibility 0.2. [Evaluation summary](../../research_evaluation/evaluation_summary.json).
9. **Does a higher risk score mean higher failure risk?** No. The displayed 0–100 number is favorability; higher is more favorable under the existing rules. [Risk implementation](../../backend/engine/risk_signals.py).
10. **Why did the Random Forest score 100%?** Its reconstructed holdout labels were generated by deterministic rules from related review features, so agreement does not validate future failures. [Model evaluation](../../research_evaluation/model_evaluation.md).
11. **Was the original holdout exactly reproduced?** Preprocessing and split procedure were reconstructed, but the original held-out row IDs were not stored. [Model evaluation](../../research_evaluation/model_evaluation.md).
12. **Does compatibility prove two vendors worked together?** No. Production matrix values describe service-category pairs. Vendor-pair evidence is separate. [Matrix](../../vendor_compatibility_matrix.csv), [Task 2](../pp1_task2_conflict_engine/README.md).
13. **Why can compatibility scores tie?** Packages with the same required service categories can share the same category-pair score regardless of vendor identities. [Compatibility module](../../backend/engine/service_compatibility.py).
14. **What does VERIFICATION_REQUIRED mean?** Recorded checks may pass in simulation, but essential availability, capacity confirmation, scheduling, collaboration or venue evidence is missing. [Feasibility implementation](../../backend/engine/dynamic_feasibility.py).
15. **Does PASS_IN_SIMULATION certify a booking?** No. It describes only checks supported by simulation records. [Task 5 risks](../pp1_task5_demo_readiness/remaining_risks.md).
16. **Can FAIL packages be recommended?** The production path validates examined combinations before final rank/selection and excludes FAIL. [Bounded generator](../../backend/engine/bounded_package_generator.py).
17. **What did Task 2 prove?** Correct deterministic statuses for 12 synthetic fixtures, including 6 known conflicts and 4 pending cases; no real-world accuracy. [Task 2 evaluation](../pp1_task2_conflict_engine/evaluation_report.md).
18. **Is Task 2 in production?** No. It is standalone; Task 3 is read-only shadow integration. [Task 3 README](../pp1_task3_shadow_integration/README.md).
19. **Why did Task 3 find zero conflicts?** All six packages lacked event-specific supporting evidence, so zero supported conflicts is not evidence of no real conflicts. [Task 3 evaluation](../pp1_task3_shadow_integration/evaluation_report.md).
20. **Was the five-second target achieved?** Not established. Task 4 had some prototype runs above five seconds and only two runs per scenario; no deployment. [Task 4 report](../pp1_task4_performance/performance_report.md).
21. **What baseline was used?** Cost-only reranking of the same examined package pool, which isolates ranking effects. It is not Criteria-Based Individual Vendor Selection. [Historical methodology](../../research_evaluation/evaluation_methodology.md).
22. **Is the proposed ranking proven superior?** No. It optimizes its own weighted criteria and costs more on average in the comparable historical events; no independent user-choice ground truth exists. [Evaluation summary](./evaluation_summary.md).
23. **Are LKR prices vendor quotations?** No. They are synthetic service-option estimates. [Task 1 audit](../audit_2026-10-17_01/audit_summary.md).
24. **What happens when search truncates?** The API reports candidate/search limits; the returned top-K covers examined combinations only. [Generator](../../backend/engine/bounded_package_generator.py).
25. **How is a dynamic feasibility submission protected?** Backend resolves option IDs against source rows and rejects conflicting submitted material fields; Task 5 observed HTTP 422 for a tampered price. [Task 5 results](../pp1_task5_demo_readiness/demo_readiness_report.md).
26. **Where is the venue component?** Another team owns it. This component documents an input/output boundary but has no active Digital Twin check. [Venue contract](../audit_2026-10-17_01/venue_integration_contract.md).
27. **How many research scenarios were evaluated?** The historical local evaluation used 35 synthetic scenarios, 34 successful requests and 202 returned packages. [Summary](../../research_evaluation/evaluation_summary.json).
28. **What is the next defensible research step?** Gather independently verified vendor and operational outcomes, implement a true individual-vendor baseline, and evaluate choice quality and conflicts against independent labels. [Limitations](./limitations_and_future_work.md).

# Limitations and future work

- **Synthetic option data:** LKR prices, capacity, location support, availability and service details are simulation estimates. Obtain sourced, consented/current quotes and operational evidence before any booking claim. [Task 1 audit](../audit_2026-10-17_01/audit_summary.md).
- **Model validity:** existing risk labels are deterministic rules on related review features. Reconstructed perfect holdout agreement cannot establish prediction of future vendor failures. Original held-out row IDs were not retained. Seek independent outcome labels, leakage-safe split and external validation. [Model evaluation](../../research_evaluation/model_evaluation.md).
- **Compatibility:** the 15 matrix pairs describe service categories. Same required categories can give packages identical scores; no proven vendor collaboration follows. Gather timestamped, source-backed pair evidence and retain unknown/pending semantics. [Task 2](../pp1_task2_conflict_engine/limitations.md).
- **Feasibility:** for new USER events, availability, vendor collaboration, travel/setup scheduling, capacity confirmation and venue suitability need outside verification. PASS_IN_SIMULATION would remain a simulation result. Venue component is external. [Task 3](../pp1_task3_shadow_integration/limitations.md).
- **Search and performance:** 12 candidates/service and 5,000 nodes can truncate search; a top-ranked package is not a global optimum. Task 4 optimization is not deployed and did not establish consistent sub-five-second latency. [Task 4](../pp1_task4_performance/performance_report.md).
- **Evaluation:** cost-only reranking is not the proposal's individual-vendor selection baseline. No independent package-choice ground truth, user study/SUS, deployed performance, real vendor outcome validation or real conflict accuracy. Proposal FR wording is provisional until the official PDF is supplied. [Task 1 gaps](../audit_2026-10-17_01/proposal_gap_analysis.md), [Task 5 risks](../pp1_task5_demo_readiness/remaining_risks.md).

Next milestones: secure original proposal and real-data provenance; design a true individual-vendor baseline and independent choice/conflict ground truth; verify vendor evidence; integrate Task 2/3 only after production design and regression review; measure repeated end-to-end latency; obtain the external venue-team contract and test its boundary.

## Decisions supported by the measurements

1. Preserve the current working data and PP1 backup. The live model/features and active foreign keys do not need replacement.
2. Start with a **versioned location-label mapping and provenance crosswalk** after PP1 because those changes are checkable without inventing service offerings.
3. Then review sparse services against original category evidence. Any added options remain explicitly synthetic, require a documented generation method, and need API/model regression checks.
4. Treat budget/capacity rebalancing and service-area or availability expansion as research-design changes. They need separate approval and evidence; they are not routine cleaning.
5. Do not loosen the candidate cap or compatibility/feasibility rules to make no-match events produce packages.

A completely new dataset would only be warranted for a different objective: independently verified vendor offerings, quotes, capacities, service areas and availability. The current source cannot be transformed into those facts by cleaning alone.

## Confirmed architecture and component responsibilities

React [App](../../frontend/src/App.jsx) and [API client](../../frontend/src/services/api.js) submit USER requests. FastAPI [request schema](../../backend/schemas/dynamic_package_request.py) validates type/date/time/service/budget. [Dynamic service](../../backend/engine/dynamic_package_service.py) loads current synthetic options, creates `USER-*`, filters candidates, gets risk and compatibility signals, and calls [bounded search](../../backend/engine/bounded_package_generator.py). [Feasibility](../../backend/engine/dynamic_feasibility.py) checks each complete candidate before final ranking; [explanations](../../backend/engine/package_explanations.py) summarize actual evidence. The external venue component is responsible for venue evidence; see [contract](./venue_integration_contract.md). Historical evaluation is in [research_evaluation](../../research_evaluation/).

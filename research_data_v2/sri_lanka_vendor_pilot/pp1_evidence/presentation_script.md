# PP1 presentation script: Sri Lankan vendor dataset pilot

**Show:** [listing evidence table](./listing_evidence.csv), then [category and field-gap tables](./pp1_evidence_report.md), then the previously verified Philadelphia prototype pages.

> “PlanoVision currently demonstrates its recommendation method with historical US Yelp-derived profiles and synthetic service options. The existing Philadelphia Wedding request previously generated three explained packages, but its simulated options do not prove real availability.
>
> For the future Sri Lankan market, I collected a separate, ODbL-licensed OpenStreetMap pilot of ten source-linked listings: seven tagged photographers and three tagged caterers. I opened all ten OSM element pages; their names, category tags and mapped cities match the CSV. These are mapped listings, **not verified bookable event vendors**. The sample has no confirmed prices, capacities, service areas, availability or review features, and it has no qualifying records yet for Decoration, Entertainment or Audio Visual.
>
> The validator passed the ten-row sample, but zero rows are ready for the existing Random Forest and zero have the priced service options required for package generation. The saved model was trained to match rule-derived review-risk labels, so I would not apply it to these OSM rows or claim it measures Sri Lankan vendor reliability.
>
> My next research step is a permission-based vendor intake for scoped LKR service quotes, supported locations, capacity and event-type evidence. I will test any future adapter separately before changing the working prototype.” 

**If asked “Are these real vendors?”** “They are real OSM map listings whose names, city tags and broad category tags were source-checked. Current operation, event service, capacity, price and availability were not independently verified.”

**If asked “Why no Sri Lankan packages?”** “The package engine needs priced service-option rows, supported event types and confirmed service locations. The OSM pilot provides business profiles, not those option records.”
